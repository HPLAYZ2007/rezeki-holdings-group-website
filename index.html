About Us

The meaning behind the name

Rezeki — a word meaning sustenance, fortune, and growth. It's the philosophy at the heart of everything we do.

Rezeki Holdings Group is an early-stage financial consulting firm that partners with pre-revenue startups and student founders to manage their finances, control cash burn, and make smarter capital decisions.

Unlike traditional consultants, we take equity alongside our retainer fees, meaning we grow with the companies we serve. When you win, we win. That's not a tagline. That's our business model.

Based in Toronto, we serve founders across Canada and beyond, from hackathon teams and SaaS builders to early-stage ventures ready to scale.

Why Rezeki

Built different.
By design.

01

Founder-Focused Approach

We are founders too. We understand the weight of decisions made with incomplete information, on too little sleep, with too much at stake. Our advice comes from lived experience, not a textbook.

02

Affordable for Early-Stage Startups

A full-time CFO costs $150K–$200K per year. A fractional CFO costs $5K–$10K per month. We start at $100. Pre-revenue founders deserve real financial oversight without burning their runway to get it.

03

Real Insight, Not Generic Advice

AI tools can generate reports. They cannot challenge a pricing assumption in a live founder meeting, or sit across from an investor and defend your model. We can. That's the difference.

Built for Long-Term Wealth
